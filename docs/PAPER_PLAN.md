# Paper Plan

Evidence will be accumulated during the competition rather than reconstructed afterward.

Planned manuscript evidence includes the dataset and validation protocol, custom architecture, physical-scale modelling, multi-scale and multi-view modules, constrained GSD head, baseline comparisons, ablation study, multi-seed robustness, geotechnical secondary metrics, error analysis, limitations, and reproducibility statement.

A leaderboard result alone is not treated as scientific evidence.
